Hey there! I'm Mojtaba Turkmani, but pretty much everyone just calls me Mo. I'm an electrical engineer who still gets a kick out of taking things apart to see how they work. Right now I'm an Application Engineer at Keysight Technologies, helping customers sort out RF testing problems.

How I got here

My path wasn't a straight line. I had some rough patches early on, and honestly they're a big part of why I don't give up easily. I leaned hard into school, especially math and science, because I loved the feeling of cracking a tough problem.

The real spark came my freshman year of high school, when I started tinkering with electronics. I spent hours fixing and tinkering with whatever I could get my hands on. In college I started in Computer Engineering, then switched to Accounting because I thought it would be easier. That lasted until I realized I was selling myself short. So I jumped into Electrical Engineering, graduated from San Diego State University, and haven't looked back since.

What I do now

At Keysight I support customers with RF testing and troubleshooting. When a problem can't be solved over the phone, I hop on a plane and work it out on site with their team. Before that, I spent a few years as an electrical engineer at L3Harris in Utah, building and fixing automated test systems.

The common thread through every job? I like finding the root cause, making things more reliable, and automating the boring parts.

Outside of work

I care a lot about growing as a person and helping others do the same, whether that's mentoring someone or sharing what I know on YouTube. When I'm off the clock you'll usually find me outdoors, camping, or hanging out with my wife and kiddo.

Thanks for stopping by and getting to know me a little!
